QUANTUM APPS

Free, open-source Android apps. Each one puts a handful of sites you already use behind a single front door, and blocks the ads, popups and redirects on the way.

WRITTEN BY AI

Every app here was written by AI — Claude Code — including this page. The complete source for each one is public on GitHub. Read it before you install an APK, from me or from anyone. Nothing here collects analytics, telemetry or accounts.

THE APPS

Android 7.0 or newer. Installed by downloading the APK, not through the Play Store.

QUANTUM MTG

MAGIC: THE GATHERING
  • Three sites, one app. Moxfield for deckbuilding, TCGplayer for buying cards, XMage Nexus for playing online. Switch from a drawer on the right edge. Browsing needs no account.
  • Blocks ads, popups and redirects. Three layers, the way real blockers work: a 370-domain blocklist matched against every request and its parent domains, URL pattern filters, and CSS element hiding. Popup windows never open.
  • The sites still work. Blanket ad rules break single-page apps, so full-screen overlays are only removed when the element itself looks like an ad. Moxfield's deck editor, card previews and the TCGplayer cart survive.
  • Card images load. Moxfield serves card art from Scryfall's CDN on a different domain to the site itself. That CDN is explicitly allowed through, so pictures are never blanked out.
  • Desktop-site toggle. Moxfield's deck editor and XMage Nexus are laid out for wide screens. One tap swaps the user agent and reloads.
  • Screen filters and text zoom. Eye comfort in two strengths, stay-awake blue, extra dark, sepia, grayscale, and text size controls.
  • Sign-in works. OAuth popups would be swallowed by the popup blocker, so they are turned into ordinary top-level redirects.

Two limits worth knowing. Google blocks its sign-in flow inside every Android WebView, so use email or Discord on Moxfield. And XMage Nexus is the community and download site — the games themselves run in the separate XMage desktop client.

QUANTUMMANGA

MANGA, LIGHT NOVELS, FANFIC
  • Seven sites, one app. MangaFire and MangaDex for manga, NovelUpdates and Novelous for light novels, RoyalRoad and HoneyFeed for web novels, Archive of Our Own for fanfic.
  • Blocks ads, popups and redirects. The same three-layer blocking, which on manga sites is most of the point: no popunders, no tab hijacks, no redirect chains between chapters.
  • Image CDNs stay allowed. Manga pages are served from rotating CDN domains, which the blocker lets through, so pages are not left half-loaded.
  • Reading filters and text zoom. Eye comfort in two strengths, sepia, extra dark, grayscale, stay-awake blue, and text size controls for novel sites.
  • Links out still work. NovelUpdates sends you to translator sites by design, so outbound reading links are not intercepted.

INSTALLING

Same three steps for every app here.

  1. Tap DOWNLOAD APK. The file lands in your downloads folder.
  2. Open it. Android asks whether to allow installs from your browser — allow it. You only do this once.
  3. Tap Install, then open the app.

Android may warn that the app is from an unknown developer. That warning appears for everything installed outside the Play Store, including this. The source is public if you want to check what you are installing, or build it yourself instead.
